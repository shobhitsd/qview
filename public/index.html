QView Quantum Readiness User Guide

Enterprise Handbook: Discovery, Scoring Models, Mosca's Inequality, and PQC Migration

1. What is QView & CBOM?

QView is an enterprise Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) and Quantum Readiness platform. It performs deterministic static code analysis, certificate audits, dependency checks, and network protocol discovery to identify quantum-vulnerable cryptography (RSA, ECC, Diffie-Hellman) without LLM hallucinations.

2. Understanding the 3 Core Quantitative Indices

QRI (Quantum Readiness Index)

A 0 - 100 composite readiness score. Measures your organization's transition progress across 8 weighted dimensions, multiplied by your scan coverage factor ($QRI = Base \times Coverage$). Higher is better (Target: 90+).

QEI (Quantum Exposure Index)

A 0 - 100 cumulative attack surface score. Measures asset vulnerability to Shor's and Grover's algorithms, data confidentiality lifetime, and business criticality. Lower is better (Target: <20).

CAI (Crypto Agility Index)

A 0 - 100 agility score. Assesses how easily your cryptographic code can be updated without major architectural rewrites. Penalizes hardcoding and rewards provider abstraction.

3. Mosca's Inequality & HNDL Risk

Mosca's theorem dictates that if $X + Y > Z$, an organization is vulnerable to Harvest Now, Decrypt Later (HNDL) attacks today:

  • X (Data Shelf-Life): How long your sensitive data (EHR, PCI, PII, intellectual property) must remain confidential.
  • Y (Migration Time): The estimated years required to re-engineer, test, and deploy PQC across all systems.
  • Z (Time to Quantum Threat): The estimated years until Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computers (CRQCs) emerge (typically 7 years).

4. Navigating the 7 Platform Views

1. Executive Overview: High-level CISO telemetry, animated score gauges, 8-dimension spider radar chart, and real-time Mosca risk simulator.

3. Quantum Heatmap: 4x4 matrix plotting business criticality against quantum exposure with instant cell drill-down.
4. Compliance Radar: Real-time breach mapping for NIST SP 800-131A, HIPAA, PCI-DSS 4.0, SEBI CSCRF, and CNSA 2.0.
5. Migration Cockpit: NIST FIPS 203/204/205 Wave 0–4 transition roadmap with step-by-step code playbooks.
6. Knowledge Graph: Interactive 2D network canvas visualizing cryptographic dependencies.
7. Evidence Center: IDE-style source code viewer with line provenance and AST semantic verification.
